Privacy Policy

At VibeTube, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our social media platform.

By using VibeTube, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Information We Collect

Personal Information:

  • Username - Your unique identifier on the platform
  • Email - Used for login and account recovery (hidden from other users)
  • Profile Information - Name, bio, profile photo, cover photo
  • Content - Posts, comments, stories, reels you upload

Usage Data:

  • Posts you like, share, and save
  • People you follow and interact with
  • Your activity on the platform
  • Device information (browser, operating system)
  • IP address and location data

How We Use Your Information

  • Provide and Maintain Services - Operate, maintain, and improve VibeTube
  • Personalize Experience - Show content relevant to your interests
  • Connect You - Enable interactions with other users
  • Monetization - Manage points, rewards, and withdrawals
  • Security - Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and spam
  • Communications - Send notifications and updates about the platform

Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using:

  • Firebase Firestore - For all text data and user profiles
  • Firebase Authentication - For secure login credentials
  • Cloudinary - For video and media storage
  • ImgBB - For image storage

All data is encrypted in transit and at rest using industry-standard encryption protocols.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. However, we may share data:

  • With Your Consent - When you explicitly allow us to share
  • Service Providers - For platform operations (Cloudinary, ImgBB, Firebase)
  • Legal Requirements - If required by law or to protect our rights
  • Public Content - Posts, comments, and profile information you make public

Your Rights & Controls

  • View - Access your profile and activity data
  • Edit - Update your profile information anytime
  • Delete - Delete your account and all associated data
  • Hide - Use hidden chats for private conversations
  • Withdraw - Request payout of your earnings

⚠️ Account Deletion: When you delete your account, all your data including posts, comments, messages, and profile information will be permanently removed from our systems within 30 days.
